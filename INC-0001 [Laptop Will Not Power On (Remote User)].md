## User Report
"I work the Customer Support queue and I'm on a work-from-home day, but my laptop won't power on at all. No lights, no fan, nothing when I hold the power button. I've got customers waiting and I can't sign into the softphone without it. Can you get me a replacement?"

## Business Impact
The user was a Customer Support agent working remotely and was unable to access the company's softphone, Microsoft Teams, or other business applications. As the user was responsible for handling customer calls, the incident was classified as Priority 1 (Critical) due to the immediate impact on customer service operations.

## Resolution Process
1) Troubleshooting Review - the following checks had already been completed by the user:

-Held the power button for several seconds.
-Tested two known-good power adapters.
-Connected the laptop to a different wall outlet.
-Verified that there were still no LEDs, fan noise, or display activity.

2) Remote Hardware Assessment - The fault was suspected to be an internal hardware failure involving the battery, DC power circuitry, or motherboard, based on the symptoms and troubleshooting already completed.

3) Issue Replacement - the user was unable to work remotely and supported a customer-facing department, restoring service quickly was prioritised over time consuming hardware diagnostics, A replacement device was approved to minimise downtime.company laptop from available inventory and arranged shipment to the user's home address. 

4) Receive Faulty Device - The faulty laptop was scheduled for return to the IT department for further hardware diagnostics and repair.

5) Device Setup - After the replacement laptop was received, assisted the user with:

Signing into Windows.
Connecting to the company VPN.
Signing into Microsoft 365.
Configuring Microsoft Teams.
Verifying the softphone application was operational.
Confirming access to required business systems.

Confirmed the replacement laptop was functioning correctly and that the user could successfully connect to the VPN, access Microsoft Teams, sign into the softphone application, and resume handling customer calls. 

Ticket Closed. INC-001 Resolved.
