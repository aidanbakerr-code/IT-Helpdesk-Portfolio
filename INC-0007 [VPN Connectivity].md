##User Report

The user reported that they were working remotely and were unable to connect to the company's VPN. The user confirmed that their home internet connection was working normally but they were unable to access internal company systems required for their work.

##Business Impact

The user was unable to access internal company resources while working remotely. Continued VPN failure would prevent the user from accessing required business systems and could delay scheduled Sales activities.

##Resolution Process

1) Troubleshooting Performed - User confirmed that their home internet connection was working and that they could access external websites.

User confirmed that the VPN connection had previously worked successfully from the same device.

User attempted to reconnect to the VPN but received an authentication/connection failure.

2) Reviewed the reported issue and confirmed that the problem appeared to be isolated to the user's VPN connection rather than their general internet connection.

Confirmed that the user was using the correct company VPN client and VPN profile.

3) Checked the workstation's date and time settings to ensure they were correct, as incorrect system time can interfere with authentication.

Confirmed that the user's system date and time were correct.

4) Confirmed that the user's account was active and authorised for remote access. Verified that the user was using the correct company credentials.

Closed the VPN client and restarted the application.

5) Re-established the VPN connection and requested the user authenticate again using their company credentials.

The VPN connection was successfully established.

6) Confirmed that the workstation had received a VPN network connection and tested access to an internal company resource.

The internal resource was accessible successfully.

7) User confirmed that they could access the required internal systems and continue working remotely.

The user was advised to connect to the company VPN before accessing internal resources while working remotely.

Ticket Closed. INC-0007 Resolved.
