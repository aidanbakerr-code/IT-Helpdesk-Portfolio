## User Report
Following a brief power outage, all users on Floor 1 lost internet connectivity. Both wired and wireless devices are unable to access network resources. Users located on other floors continue to have normal network access.

## Business Impact
30 users on Floor 1 were unable to access the internet or internal company resources. Engineering staff were unable to perform their duties, resulting in a complete interruption of work across the affected floor.

## Resolution Process
1) Confirmed the issue affected all users on Floor 1 while users on other floors remained connected. This indicated the issue was isolated to the Floor 1 network infrastructure rather than a company-wide internet outage.

2) Accessed the Server Room management console to review the status of network infrastructure supporting Floor 1.

3) Opened the Devices section and located the Floor 1 Switch, which was suspected to have failed to recover correctly following the power interruption.

4) Performed a controlled reboot of the Floor 1 Switch to restore normal network services after the power event.

5) Contacted the reporting user through Microsoft Teams and requested confirmation that network connectivity had been restored. The user confirmed internet access had returned and that both wired and wireless devices were functioning normally.


Ticket Closed. INC-0003 Resolved.
